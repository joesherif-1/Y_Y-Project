What happens here?

  1. Hand photo + AI spot check Optional
    Take a photo, tap a mole or spot, and our neural network compares that exact spot with dermatology images it trained on. Can add up to 3 points to your score.
  2. Quick hand self-check Optional
    Your own eyes check for pale, yellow, or shape changes the camera can't judge. Can add up to 8 points.
  3. Questionnaire Required
    Seven short sections about you, your history, lifestyle, and symptoms — then your score and analysis.

Nothing is uploaded — photos and answers stay on your device until you submit the questionnaire, and photos never leave it at all.
